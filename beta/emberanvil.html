Ember & Anvil

Beta

Ember & Anvil is an idle blacksmith RPG for iPhone and iPad. You run a forge at the edge of the dungeon: gather and refine materials, craft and enchant gear, and equip hero parties for their expeditions. No pay-to-win — no ads, no in-app purchases, nothing to buy.

What we want feedback on

Bug reports are welcome, but these four questions are what the beta is actually for:

  • Does progress feel like progress? Idle games live or die on this. Where did it feel like you were getting somewhere, and where did it start to feel like waiting for a number to go up?
  • Is it clear what items are for? When you pick up an ore, a log or a rare find, do you know what it is good for and what to do with it next — or does it just pile up in the inventory?
  • Balancing. Which stretches dragged and which went too fast? Rough numbers help: your total level and shaft depth at the point where it tipped.
  • Handling. Did you find the thing you were looking for? Anything that took too many taps, any screen where you weren't sure what to do, anything that felt wrong on your device size — iPhone, iPad or iPhone Duo.

What's in the beta

  • Four gathering skills — mining, woodcutting, foraging and trapping — each with its own actions and rare finds
  • A shaft that goes deeper with every mining cycle, through depth tiers that unlock new ores
  • Chambers uncovered as you dig, and delvers you equip and send into them
  • A village of workplaces that refine and craft while you are away
  • Offline progress: the game catches up when you return, for up to 12 hours per absence
  • Layouts for iPhone, iPad and the foldable iPhone Duo, following the hinge as it opens
  • Plays entirely offline — no account, no network requests, no ads, no purchases

Requirements

Ember & Anvil requires iOS or iPadOS 27 or later and runs on iPhone and iPad. Your save game stays on the device — it is not synchronised between devices yet, and the gear menu has a reset so you can start over at any time.
