Sketchline

Beta

Sketchline is a natural drawing app for iPhone and iPad. It is live on the App Store — version 1.3.1 is currently in testing on TestFlight, so you can try the latest changes before they ship.

Join the beta on TestFlight

Tap the button below to join via TestFlight. You'll need the free TestFlight app installed on your iPhone or iPad.

TestFlight is Apple's free beta testing platform. The beta may contain bugs — please report them via the TestFlight feedback button or by email.

What's new in this beta

Version 1.3.1 · Build 49

  • Siri and App Intents. Start a new drawing or open your most recent one by voice, and add the same actions to Shortcuts.
  • iPhone support. The full drawing surface, gallery and toolbar now adapt to iPhone, not just iPad.
  • iPhone Duo split view. On the foldable iPhone Duo the gallery and canvas sit side by side on the inner display, and the layout follows the hinge as it opens and closes.
  • Fixed: the app no longer freezes on launch. Opening the app could hang on the first frame while restoring the last drawing. Startup is now reliable.

What's in Sketchline

  • 9 natural brush types: pencil, fountain pen, watercolor, crayon, marker, monoline, and more
  • Full Apple Pencil support on iPad including hover preview, barrel roll, double-tap tool switching, and Squeeze (Apple Pencil Pro)
  • Import photos as reference layers with background removal via Vision
  • Export as PNG, PDF, or native .sketchline package (can be opened from Files.app)
  • Minimal, distraction-free UI — the canvas is the interface
  • Multi-drawing gallery with auto-save and full undo/redo
  • Siri and Shortcuts actions for starting and reopening drawings

Requirements

Sketchline requires iOS or iPadOS 26 or later and runs on iPhone and iPad. An Apple Pencil is optional — it is fully supported on iPad, including hover preview and Apple Pencil Pro, but not required.
