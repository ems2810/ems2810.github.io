What's in Sketchline

  • 9 natural brush types: pencil, fountain pen, watercolor, crayon, marker, monoline, and more
  • Full Apple Pencil support including hover preview, barrel roll, double-tap tool switching, and Squeeze (Apple Pencil Pro)
  • Import photos as reference layers with background removal via Vision
  • Export as PNG, PDF, or native .sketchline package (can be opened from Files.app)
  • Minimal, distraction-free UI — the canvas is the interface
  • Multi-drawing gallery with auto-save and full undo/redo

Requirements

Sketchline requires an iPad running iPadOS 17 or later. An Apple Pencil is strongly recommended.
