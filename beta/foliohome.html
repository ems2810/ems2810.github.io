Folio Home

Beta Testing

Folio Home is a household budget and finance app for iPhone and iPad, currently in active development. The beta runs on iOS 26 and is distributed via TestFlight.

Dashboard

Income, expenses, weekly budget and budget progress at a glance

Recurring entries

Fixed costs and savings with flexible intervals and monthly overrides

Salary calculator

Gross-to-net calculator with bundled tax data for DE, AT, CH, NL, FR, GB, US

Multi-member

Track budgets and transactions across a whole household

Annual planner

Year-at-a-glance view with balance forecasts per month

iCloud sync

Private CloudKit sync across all your Apple devices

Join via TestFlight

TestFlight is Apple's free beta testing platform. You'll need a free Apple ID and the TestFlight app installed on your iPhone or iPad running iOS 26.

Open in TestFlight →

The beta may contain bugs. Please use the in-app feedback button or email to report issues.

What to test

In the current beta, focus on:

  • Adding recurring expenses and checking month rollover
  • The salary calculator with your country's tax settings
  • Creating transactions and viewing the dashboard
  • iCloud sync across multiple devices if you have them

Feedback

Use the TestFlight feedback button, or email gourd_pulp.9b@icloud.com. Please include the iOS version and a description of what you expected vs. what happened.

Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad running iOS / iPadOS 26 or later
  • TestFlight app installed
  • Apple ID
