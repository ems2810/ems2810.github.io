Frequently asked questions

Where is my financial data stored?

All data is stored locally on your device using SwiftData. If you enable iCloud sync, your data is synced via Apple's CloudKit private database — only accessible to you. Folio Home does not run its own servers and does not transmit your financial information to Achim Braml.

How do recurring expenses work?

Define an entry with an interval (weekly, monthly, yearly, or custom) and a start date. Folio Home generates the upcoming occurrences automatically. You can override individual months — for example, if rent changes for one month — without breaking the recurring schedule.

How accurate is the salary calculator?

The gross-to-net calculator uses bundled tax tables for DE, AT, CH, NL, FR, GB, US and other countries. Results are estimates for planning purposes only and don't replace official advice from a tax professional. Tax data is updated with app releases.

Can multiple household members share a budget?

Yes. Add each member as a profile and assign income, expenses, and shared categories. The dashboard rolls everything up to a household view, and you can drill down per member.

How do I back up my data?

Enable iCloud sync (free) for automatic backup across your Apple devices. Pro users can also configure a WebDAV / NAS endpoint for encrypted external backups, and export everything as CSV or PDF from Settings → Data.

What does Pro include?

Pro unlocks the Annual Planner with monthly balance forecasts, advanced CSV/PDF export, multiple salary snapshots, WebDAV sync, advanced analytics, and removes ads. It's available as a monthly or yearly auto-renewable subscription.

How do I delete all my data?

Open Settings → Data → Delete All Data within the app. To also clear iCloud data, go to iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Folio Home → Delete Data.

Known issues

  • Tax data updates only ship with new app versions — keep the app up to date for the most current rates.
  • Very large CSV exports (10,000+ rows) may take several seconds to generate.
  • WebDAV connection errors are sometimes reported generically — check server URL and credentials.
