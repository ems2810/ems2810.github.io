Sketchline

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026 · Applies to Sketchline for iPadOS

Overview

Sketchline is a drawing app. Your drawings stay on your device. The app does not upload your artwork or personal data to any server operated by Achim Braml.

Drawings and artwork

All drawings are stored locally on your device using SwiftData. Artwork is never transmitted to external servers. Exported files (PNG, PDF, .sketchline) go wherever you choose to send them — that is entirely within your control.

Photos library access

Sketchline may request access to your Photos library to import reference images. Photos are copied into the drawing as embedded data and the original is not modified. The app does not upload photos anywhere.

iCloud sync

A future version of Sketchline may offer iCloud sync. This section will be updated when that feature ships.

Advertising

The free tier shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers subject to your App Tracking Transparency consent. Denying tracking shows non-personalized ads. Purchasing the Remove Ads subscription removes all advertising.

See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Subscriptions

Handled by Apple via StoreKit 2. No payment data is accessible to Achim Braml.

Data deletion

Delete the app to remove all local data. No data is retained externally.

Children's privacy

Sketchline is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

Policy updates will be published at this URL with a revised date.
