Ember & Anvil

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Summary

Ember & Anvil does not collect any data. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind, and it does not contain third-party SDKs. Your save game stays on your device.

Who is responsible

Achim Braml — see the Impressum for the full postal address and contact details. Questions about this policy: gourd_pulp.9b@icloud.com

What the app stores, and where

Ember & Anvil keeps a single save game: your skill levels and experience, your inventory, the depth of your shaft, the chambers you have uncovered, your village buildings and your delvers and their equipment. It also stores a randomly generated device identifier that is used only to tell save files apart, plus the timestamp of the last save.

This save game is written as a file inside the app's own container on your device (Application Support), together with one backup copy of the previous save. Nobody but you and the app can read it. It is included in your device backups — an encrypted iPhone or iPad backup, or an iCloud device backup — because iOS backs up the app container; that backup is governed by Apple's terms and your own iCloud settings, not by the app.

The app does not currently synchronise your save game between devices. The game lives on one device at a time.

What the app does not do

  • No network access. The app makes no network requests. It contains no code that talks to a server — not mine, not anyone else's.
  • No advertising. There is no ad SDK and there are no ads.
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting SDK. No usage statistics, no event logging, no attribution.
  • No tracking. The app does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, and does not combine your data with data from other sources.
  • No account, no sign-in, no e-mail address collected.
  • No third-party SDKs. The app uses only Apple's own frameworks that ship with iOS.
  • No in-app purchases and no subscription in the current version.

Under Apple's App Privacy categories, the app's declaration is Data Not Collected.

Deleting your data

The gear menu inside the game has a reset that discards the save game and starts over. Deleting the app from your device removes the save game and its backup copy along with it. Because nothing leaves your device, there is no server-side copy for me to delete and nothing for me to hand over.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children. It contains no ads, no social features and no links that lead out of the app other than the support e-mail address.

TestFlight beta

While Ember & Anvil is distributed as a beta through Apple's TestFlight, Apple processes your participation as a tester — the e-mail address you joined with, your device and iOS version, installation and crash data, and any feedback or screenshots you send through TestFlight. That happens on Apple's side under Apple's Privacy Policy, not through anything in the app. As the developer I can see aggregated tester, installation and crash information in App Store Connect, and the feedback you choose to send. If you e-mail me directly, I process your message and address only to answer you, and I delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to complain to a supervisory authority. Since the app itself collects nothing, these rights are relevant in practice only to e-mail you send me and to the TestFlight data Apple holds. Write to the address above and I will answer.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds iCloud synchronisation, in-app purchases or any other feature that handles data differently, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will change.
