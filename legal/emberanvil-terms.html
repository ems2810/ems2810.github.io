Beta software

While Ember & Anvil is distributed through TestFlight it is pre-release software. It may contain bugs, balancing is still being tuned, and features may change or be removed before release. A beta build can stop working when its testing period expires. Participation in the beta is voluntary and free, and it can be ended by you at any time from the TestFlight app, or by me at any time without notice.

Your save game

Your save game is stored on your own device and is not synchronised to any server. Save files carry a schema version and are migrated forward where possible, but during the beta a save game may become unreadable by a newer build. Keep that in mind before investing a long run — the game menu contains a reset so you can start over. No warranty of any kind is given that progress will survive an update.

Fair play and no pay-to-win

The current version of Ember & Anvil contains no in-app purchases, no subscription, no advertising and no currency that can be bought with real money. Progress comes from playing. Should a future version ever add a purchase, it will not sell progress or power.
